# Personal hard-negative prompts for astra_ml.data.ww_record --negative.

# Say each line naturally, at the same distance and in the same room as the
# matching positive session. Never say the wake word in a negative session.

# recorded across several sittings — the recorder resumes where it left off.

# Four groups, roughly in decreasing order of how much they matter:
#   1. the adversarial_phrases list in your own voice (TTS already covers the
#      phrases; what is missing is your channel saying them)
#   2. phonetic neighbourhood — stressed /æ/-/ɑ/ onsets, str/st clusters, -ra endings
#   3. connected speech, because that is the deployment condition
#   4. counting and filler, the highest-frequency things a mic actually hears
